About Our Guide

The Dragon Boat Regatta Guide is a resource designed to help paddlers, teams, and spectators discover dragon boat racing events happening throughout British Columbia and beyond. Whether you are an experienced paddler, a beginner interested in joining a team, or someone looking to experience the excitement of a regatta, our guide makes it easier to find events.
